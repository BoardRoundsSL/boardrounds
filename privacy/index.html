Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

BoardRounds is a study game for medical students. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use third-party advertising or tracking tools.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see your password).
  • Profile: your nickname, avatar choices, BoardRounds ID, time zone, and settings.
  • Study progress: boards played, answers you mark right or wrong, XP, streaks, missions, lessons, league results, and clues saved for review.
  • Friends and invites: who you've added as a friend, and who invited you (if anyone).
  • Error reports: anything you write when you report a problem with a clue.
  • Usage: how many minutes you spend in the app and how many boards you finish each day, used to improve the app.
  • Subscriptions: if you subscribe, we receive your plan, renewal dates, and price from Apple through our payments provider (RevenueCat). We never receive your card or payment details.
  • Clue statistics: anonymous, combined counts of how often each clue is answered correctly, used to set difficulty. These are not linked to you.

What other players can see

Your nickname, avatar, BoardRounds ID, streak, and XP appear on leaderboards and to your friends. Your email address is never shown to other players.

How we use it

To run your account and the game, keep your progress in sync, power friends, leagues and invites, provide Premium features, prevent cheating and abuse, answer support requests, and understand which features help people study.

Who processes it

We use a small number of service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in), Apple (the App Store and payments), RevenueCat (subscription status), and GitHub Pages (hosting this website). They process data only to provide their services to us.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or your device settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, progress, friends and usage records. A few records are kept without your identity: error reports (so clues can be fixed), anonymous clue statistics, Founding Member numbers (which are never reissued), and purchase records we must keep for accounting and tax purposes. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple account settings.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and our database only accepts requests from our own server, which checks every account's permissions.

Children

BoardRounds is intended for adults preparing for medical exams and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Changes

If we make important changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.
