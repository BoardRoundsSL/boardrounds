Support

Need help with BoardRounds? Email support@boardrounds.app and we'll get back to you, usually within 2 business days.

Common questions

How do I play?
Open the app and tap How to play on Home for a full guide, including streaks, Shift Covers, Redo mistakes and leagues.

I forgot my password.
On the Log in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the email link. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

How do I add a friend?
Ask for their BoardRounds ID (under their profile picture), then go to Friends → Add friends.

How do I cancel Premium?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → BoardRounds.

I bought Premium but don't see it.
In the app, open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't help, email us.

A clue is wrong.
Tap Report an issue under the clue. Confirmed reports earn XP.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Delete account. See our Privacy Policy for what's removed.
